Since 1994

A courtyard kitchen, three generations deep

Dabali began as a single coal stove in a family courtyard in Bhaktapur. What started as Saturday feasts for relatives grew, recipe by recipe, into a restaurant that still cooks the way our grandmothers did — buffalo smoked over coal, spices ground by hand, beaten rice pounded fresh each morning.

Everything on the table is sourced from farmers within the Kathmandu Valley — the same relationships our family has kept for decades.

What sets the table

Cooked over coal

Choila and sukuti are still smoked the traditional way, not on a gas flame.

Valley-sourced

Produce and buffalo come from farms we've worked with for over a decade.

A living recipe book

Recipes passed down rather than standardized — expect small seasonal shifts.

The courtyard itself

Restored brick and carved wood, seating for both quiet dinners and large tables.
